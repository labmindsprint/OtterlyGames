What I Believe

🎯

The Bottleneck Shifts

AI will commoditize execution — FV productivity explodes, boundaries blur. The new bottleneck? Identifying the right problem and the right solution. First principles, second-order thinking.

🤖

Agents Should Earn Trust

Start with Copilot, prove value, graduate to autonomous. Our AI Deal Agent followed this exact path — assistant first, agent second, only after sellers trusted it.

🔧

AI Must Be Useable

Too many products slap "AI" on the label without solving a real pain point. I believe in AI engineering that starts with the problem — if it isn't useable, it isn't a product.
