A Mom Who Gets It

The story behind Otterly Games — from homework tears to otter-powered learning.

It started with tears — not mine, my child's. A homework worksheet with analog clocks, and a five-year-old who couldn't understand why the big hand meant minutes and the small hand meant hours. We'd been at it for twenty minutes, and we were both exhausted.

That night, I searched the App Store for help. What I found was a landscape of apps that were either paywalled, ad-riddled, data-hungry, or simply boring. Not a single one felt like it was made by someone who'd actually sat at a kitchen table with a crying child.

So I built one.

I'm not a developer by trade — I'm a mom with a laptop, a problem-solving mindset, and the kind of stubbornness that comes from wanting something better for my son. Using AI tools, late-night coding sessions, and my son as the world's most honest tester, I created Clock Master — a game that turned telling time from homework into play.

A few months later, multiplication tables arrived. Same tears, same search, same disappointment. Same solution: build it myself. Math Tank was born — tank battles powered by times tables.

Both games share the same DNA: space journeys, coins earned through play, and guided practice. You can play the full games in your browser without an account, or choose the Android apps for offline play. Game progress stays on the current device or browser; it does not sync between them.

Why Otters?

In nature, otters are the ultimate nurturing parents. A mother otter holds her baby while she sleeps, so they don't drift apart. She teaches her pup to swim by example. Otters play constantly — not because they're lazy, but because for them, play is learning.

When I needed a name for this little brand, "Otterly Games" felt right. We're a mama otter holding on tight, teaching through play, and making sure no child drifts away from the joy of learning.

Our Promise

Every feature in our apps passes one test: "Would I want this for my own child?" If the answer is no, it doesn't ship. That's our entire product strategy.

Our Values

The principles behind every pixel, every lesson, and every line of code.

🔒 Privacy by Default

Our games need no account and save progress locally. Website hosting, optional adult contact messages, and advertising in the Android apps involve other services. Our privacy policy explains the differences.

💰 Honest Monetization

Browser play has no rewarded videos or simulated ad rewards. Earned rewards remain available. The Android apps use AdMob banners and optional rewarded ads when online, with child-directed settings. No in-app purchases.

🎮 More Than One Way to Learn

Try tank battles, clock races, hands-on activities, or a paper worksheet. Different moments call for different kinds of practice, and families can choose what fits.

🤗 Gentle by Design

Start with guided lessons or untimed practice, and try timed races or battles when your child wants a challenge. Take a break whenever it helps.
