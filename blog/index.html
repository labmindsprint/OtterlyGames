Welcome to The Otter Side

The Otter Side is where we share everything we've learned on this journey from homework tears to educational game development. Written by a mom who builds learning apps for kids, our articles cover practical parenting strategies, math teaching tips, honest app reviews, and real stories about raising confident learners.

Whether you're looking for ways to make multiplication practice less painful, need advice on managing screen time, or want step-by-step guides for teaching tricky concepts like telling time or division, you'll find honest, experience-based advice here. Every article is written from the perspective of a parent who's been in the trenches — no jargon, no judgment, just practical help.

What We Write About

Parenting Tips — Actionable advice for helping children with homework, building confidence in maths, and turning everyday moments into learning opportunities. We cover topics like helping children who hate maths, making multiplication practice enjoyable, and setting up productive homework routines.

Learning Guides — Step-by-step instructions for teaching specific skills. From telling the time on analogue clocks to understanding division with remainders, each guide breaks complex topics into simple, age-appropriate steps that parents can follow at home.

App Reviews & Resources — Honest reviews of educational apps and free online tools. We test everything with real children before recommending it, and we always note the privacy and safety implications for families.

Digital Parenting — Practical guidance on screen time, educational technology, and raising digitally literate children. We share what works in our own family — the successes and the mistakes.
