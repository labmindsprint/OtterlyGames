Otter Element rigged alien enemy roster
=======================================

All models in this folder (skitter, charger, beetle, moth, stalker, bulwark,
harbinger, matriarch) are original work created for the Otter Element project.
They were generated procedurally with Blender 5.2 Python scripts
(tools/rigged-enemies/*.py); sources are in art/rigged-enemies/source/*.blend.
No third-party meshes, textures or animations are included.
Silhouettes were guided by the project's own concept images
(art/lowpoly-space/poly2/*.png). Licensed as project assets (same terms as the
Otter Element repository).
