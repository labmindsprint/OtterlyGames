For parents, teachers and partners

Help little
discoveries grow.

Otterly is a small, independently made collection. The browser games, learning tools and printable practice are free to use. Here are a few grown-up ways to help.

Share something useful

Found a tool that helps? Link to its page from a classroom resource list, parent group or school newsletter. Sharing the page keeps its instructions, privacy information and related practice together.

Explore learning paths

Tell us what needs work

Parents and teachers can send feedback about a confusing instruction, an accessibility barrier or a skill they would like to practise. Please do not include children's names, photos, schools or other identifying information.

Send adult feedback

Support the maker

Adults can choose to support development on Ko-fi. This is entirely optional: it does not unlock a child's progress, buy game rewards or change access to the free browser games.

Ko-fi is an external service with its own account, payment and privacy terms. No payment widget loads on this page.

Adults: visit Ko-fi ↗

Resource partnerships

Publishers, libraries and education organisations can enquire about a relevant, clearly labelled resource sponsorship. A useful parent guide or printable companion is a better starting point than an interruption in a child's game.

Enquiries are reviewed individually. There are no guaranteed traffic, placement or revenue figures, and an enquiry is not a sponsorship agreement.

Discuss a partnership

Advertising is a separate decision

The browser games do not include advertising SDKs or working video-ad rewards. Website ad serving and analytics are disabled in this version. Any future provider integration needs its own approval, audience treatment and applicable privacy or consent setup before it is enabled.

Optional Android apps have their own advertising and privacy details. Read the privacy policy before choosing a service.
