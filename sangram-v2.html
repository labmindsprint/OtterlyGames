How I Think

Product judgment that comes from building across hardware, strategy, and software

🎯

Kill the Feature

I removed 10+ low-ROI scenarios from an AI Agent charter and 400+ outdated rules from a catalog. Saying no is the hardest PM skill — and the most valuable.

🔬

Instrument, Then Decide

I set up Seller Think Tanks and partnered with research studios before building. Every feature starts with a quantified hypothesis, not an assumption.

⚡

Ship Small, Ship Fast

Split Amendment into Admin + Seller tracks. Split Phase 2 into parallel workstreams. Decompose big bets into small, shippable increments.

🏗️

Platform Over Feature

I don't build point solutions. I architect shared exception platforms, reusable AI skill frameworks, and standard spec templates adopted org-wide.

🤝

Earn the Right to Lead

I built a VBA tool, produced the first marketing video, triaged 274 bugs. Leaders who get their hands dirty earn engineering trust.

♻️

Retire Before You Build

15+ legacy retirements saving $1M/yr. Reducing surface area is as important as expanding it. The best new feature is often deleting the old one.
