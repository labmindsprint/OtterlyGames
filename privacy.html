Privacy Policy

Effective date: February 14, 2026 · Last updated: October 26, 2026

Otterly Games ("we," "us," or "our") makes learning games and tools for children and families. This policy explains local game saves, information processed when you visit the website or contact us, and the separate advertising services used in our Android apps.

Otter Element has its own privacy notice. Otter Element is an Android game with online player profiles and rankings, Lab Siege, rewarded ads and Google Play purchases. It is described in the Otter Element Privacy Notice, not on this page. Everything below, including what it says about accounts, cloud copies of progress, public player profiles, advertising settings and purchases, describes this website, our contact form, Clock Master, Math Tank and Space Academy. It does not describe Otter Element.

1. Our Core Principle

Our games do not require an account. Game progress is stored on your device or in your browser rather than in an Otterly Games account. Keeping progress local does not mean that visiting a website, submitting a message, following an external link, or using an ad-supported Android app involves no data processing.

2. Our Mobile Apps (Clock Master, Math Tank & Space Academy)

2.1 Accounts and Local Saves

  • Our apps do not require accounts or registration to play. The upcoming Space Academy update adds an optional grown-up purchase account, described in section 2.7; it is not a child account
  • All game progress, settings, and customization data is stored locally on your device only, including in the upcoming Space Academy update
  • We do not receive a cloud copy of your saved game progress and cannot restore a deleted local save
  • Advertising providers may process information separately, as described below

2.2 Advertising

  • Our apps use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Space Academy's ads and purchases are described separately in sections 2.5 to 2.8
  • Ads are configured with child-directed treatment and non-personalized settings. These settings are not a certification or a guarantee about every ad
  • Google may process information such as IP addresses, device and app details, and ad interactions to deliver ads, measure their performance, and prevent fraud. Technical information may be personal information under applicable rules; it is not the same as a local game save
  • The Android apps may show banners when online and offer optional rewarded videos (Clock Master and Math Tank), or only optional rewarded videos (Space Academy). They are not advertised as entirely ad-free
  • See the current Google Play listing and its Data Safety section for the installed app version
  • For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Families Policy

2.3 Children's Privacy

  • Children do not need to provide a name, email address, school, or date of birth to play our games
  • There are no game accounts, chat, or public player profiles
  • Child-directed advertising settings in the Android apps do not mean that an independent body has certified our games or every advertisement
  • If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, please ask an adult to contact us so we can review it

2.4 Child Safety Features

  • Our apps are designed for children aged 4-11 (pre-primary PP1 to Class 5), to be used with a parent or carer's supervision where needed
  • In Clock Master and Math Tank, a parental gate (a math question) helps reduce accidental access to settings. In Space Academy, the grown-ups area (including the fuel shop) opens only after pressing and holding a button for two seconds. Neither verifies a person's age or identity
  • Adults should supervise store, support, and other external links
  • No social features, chat, messaging, or user-generated content

2.5 Otterly Space Academy for Android

Otterly Space Academy is a maths and reasoning practice game for children from PP1 to Class 5 (ages 4-11). The Android app bundles the whole game and runs it offline on your device. This section applies to the Android app only; the website version is described in section 3 and has no ads and no purchases. Version note: sections 2.5 and 2.6 describe the earlier closed-test implementation and documentation, in which stores offered 5 fuel and 15 fuel packs as well as Remove ads. We do not claim here which products are actually offered in Google Play today. The upcoming parent-purchase update is not yet released and is described in sections 2.7 and 2.8; those sections apply only to a version that includes it.

  • Local saves: explorer progress, settings and fuel balances are stored on your device only, in every version. In the earlier closed-test implementation purchase status is also stored on your device only. We do not receive progress or settings, and uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them. Android's automatic app backup is turned off for this app. A backup file is only created if an adult chooses Help & saves and picks where to save it; we never receive it
  • No child accounts or personal details: no name, email address, school, date of birth, location, contacts, photos or microphone access is requested, and there is no chat or social feature. The earlier closed-test implementation had no account at all; the upcoming update adds only the optional grown-up purchase account in section 2.7
  • Rewarded ads: a child can choose to watch an optional rewarded video ad for one extra unit of fuel, at most twice a day. The app shows no banner or other ads. Ads come only from Google AdMob, are requested with child-directed treatment and under-age-of-consent settings, are limited to a G content rating, are non-personalised, and need no consent form. The app does not request the Android advertising ID. If Remove ads is active on the device, no ad is offered
  • What Google receives: when an ad loads or plays, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may process technical information such as IP address, device and operating system details, app and SDK version, ad requests and interactions, and diagnostics, to serve the ad, measure it and prevent fraud. This does not include saved progress or answers. See Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's Families guidance
  • Permissions: the app asks only for Internet access (for ads and purchases) and the network and billing permissions that the Google libraries add. It does not use location, contacts, camera or the advertising ID
  • External links: links to this website, NASA and other pages open in your browser and are covered by those sites' own policies
  • See the Data safety section of the Google Play listing for the installed version

2.6 Android In-App Purchases: earlier closed-test implementation

This section describes only the earlier closed-test implementation and its documentation. It does not state which products are actually offered in Google Play, and it does not describe the upcoming update in sections 2.7 and 2.8.

  • That earlier implementation was designed to offer optional purchases through Google Play: 5 fuel and 15 fuel packs (use-once items that add mock-test fuel and can be bought again), and Unlimited fuel · no ads (also called Remove ads, a one-time purchase that is kept for good)
  • Google Play, not Otterly Games, processes the payment and holds the payment details, purchase history and receipts. We do not receive card or payment information or the buyer's name. The app only learns, through Google Play, that a purchase succeeded so it can add the fuel or switch off ads
  • Purchases are made from the grown-ups area, which opens only after pressing and holding a button for two seconds. This is a simple guard against accidental taps and does not verify that a person is an adult. Google Play may also ask for your own Google Play authentication, and parents can add further purchase controls in Google Play Family Link and settings
  • An adult can use Restore purchases in the grown-ups area to bring back Remove ads on the same Google account. Fuel packs that have been used cannot be restored
  • Refunds and cancellations are handled under Google Play's refund policy. A request can also be sent to us through our contact form
  • Purchases are not available on the website. The website has no ads and no in-app purchases; its separate Ko-fi supporter code is described in section 3.4

2.7 Upcoming Space Academy update: optional parent purchase account

Not released yet. This section and section 2.8 describe a planned Space Academy Android update. They apply only to a version that includes the update, and only after it is released. Nothing here describes any earlier closed-test implementation. Parent purchase accounts and self-service deletion are not yet enabled for this rollout, so these features may not be usable even in a version that includes the update.

  • What changes: the update source is planned to offer only one purchase, Unlimited fuel · no ads (Remove ads), a one-time purchase. It would not include the 5 fuel and 15 fuel packs of the earlier implementation. This policy does not claim that this product is currently offered in Google Play. The price shown at purchase is the local price Google Play displays, which is authoritative; this policy does not state a price
  • Recovery code, not an email or Google sign-in: a grown-up creates an optional parent recovery code in the grown-ups area to buy or restore Remove ads on another device or after a reinstall. No Google Sign-In, name or email address is collected for this account, and the game works without it. The code works like a password: keep it private. If it is lost, the purchase cannot be recovered through the account
  • Our purchase server: the app sends the recovery code transiently, and Google Play purchase details such as the purchase token, to an Otterly Games HTTPS service hosted on Microsoft Azure so it can authenticate the parent and verify the purchase with Google Play. We do not store the raw recovery code or the raw purchase token. We store a pseudonymous parent identifier, hashed session records, and purchase records and fingerprints (product, state, ownership, test or revoked status) for restoring unlimited fuel and preventing fraud. Azure and Google Play process data in line with their own policies
  • Stays local: child profiles, answers, progress, fuel balances and settings are not sent to this service and are not part of the account. Android backup stays off
  • Google Play and ads: Google Play still handles payment, refunds and receipts; we receive no card details. Optional rewarded ads still use Google AdMob as described in section 2.5
  • Signing out removes the code from the device and ends that device's session. It does not delete records held on our server; use the deletion path in section 2.8

2.8 Deleting a parent purchase account (upcoming update)

  • Where: in the update, a grown-up can choose Delete parent purchase account in the grown-ups area, or use the Space Academy account deletion page on this website with the same recovery code. The page is for adults only and sends nothing until you submit it. The deletion service is not yet enabled for this rollout, so using the page may fail
  • What deletion does: it revokes the account's server sessions and store benefits and removes the code stored on the device. A device that is offline may keep its cached benefit until its next successful contact with the server. It does not refund a Google Play purchase (refunds follow Google Play's policy) and does not delete child profiles or progress, which stay on the device
  • What we keep, and for how long: minimal pseudonymous purchase fingerprints are retained for 30 days to prevent fraud and duplicate fulfilment, then erased automatically, and server ledger history is removed afterward. The deleted recovery code is blocked during that period; after erasure the same code could start a fresh account, but the deleted history cannot be restored. We do not keep the recovery code or raw purchase token
  • If you lose the code you cannot use the deletion page. Contact us through the form, as an adult; do not send the code or purchase details in a message

3. Our Website (otterlygames.com)

3.1 Website Requests and Learning Tools

  • Our website is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages
  • This version of the website does not automatically load Google Analytics, Google AdSense, or a Ko-fi widget. Website fonts are served with the site rather than requested from Google Fonts
  • The website is delivered through Cloudflare, which can add its Cloudflare Web Analytics script to our pages. That script reports page-load timing and basic visit details to Cloudflare (see section 3.4). The parent purchase-account deletion page asks browsers not to run it, so the recovery code is not exposed to it
  • We do not use website analytics to build individual learner profiles
  • Learning-tool answers are checked in your browser, not submitted to an Otterly Games learner database. Loading pages and assets still sends ordinary requests to our hosting provider
  • Read-aloud features use your browser or device's speech service. Voice availability and any service processing depend on that browser and device. Our tools do not record microphone input

3.2 Browser Games and Embedded Demos

The full Clock Master and Math Tank browser games save progress, earned coins, unlocks, achievements, and settings in the current browser on the current device. No account is needed, and these saves do not sync to Android, another browser, or another device.

  • Homepage and product-page previews remain separate entry points from the full browser games
  • Clearing site data removes browser saves. Private browsing may discard them when the session closes. Local storage is not a backup
  • The full games block gameplay when save storage is unavailable or fails. We cannot retrieve a save that has been cleared from your browser
  • The browser games contain no analytics or advertising SDKs. Native rewarded videos and ad-only rewards are unavailable, not simulated. Rewards earned through play remain available, and daily play limits still apply
  • Any separate website display-ad placement is disabled. Enabling one would require publisher approval and the applicable privacy and consent arrangements, with this policy updated before launch. No ad is required to start a browser game

3.3 Space Academy Practice and Backups

Space Academy stores three anonymous explorer slots in this site's IndexedDB browser storage. These contain selected grades and challenge levels, question-set settings and bookmarks, submitted answers, hint use, recent skill observations, mission history, rocket parts, paint, launch charges and space discoveries. No names, email addresses, school details or dates of birth are requested. These practice records are not transmitted to an Otterly Games learner database.

  • Storage is checked after you choose Open Space Academy. Save-dependent play pauses if storage fails, rather than treating an unsaved reward as saved
  • Game progress is separate from Clock Master and Math Tank saves, does not sync across devices, and may be lost when site data is cleared or a private session closes
  • Help & saves can download a local JSON backup of all three Academy explorers. Keep it privately: it contains practice history. A confirmed restore replaces only Academy progress. Resetting one explorer removes all of that explorer's grade journeys, not the other explorers
  • Mission links contain grade, chapter, challenge, mode and seed settings only, not answers or saved progress. Do not put personal information into link settings
  • Reports are local practice observations, not IQ scores, diagnoses or rankings. The website game has no analytics or advertising SDKs, public profiles, chat or live AI question service. The Android app's ads and purchases are covered in sections 2.5 to 2.8

3.4 Third-Party Services on the Website

  • GitHub Pages: Our hosting provider. GitHub may collect standard server access logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) as part of their hosting service. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Cloudflare: Delivers our website and may add Cloudflare Web Analytics, a script that loads from static.cloudflareinsights.com and sends performance timing with the page path (without query strings), referring site, country, browser, operating system and device type. Cloudflare states that Web Analytics collects the minimum timing information and does not track individual end users across customers' sites; it keeps unsampled beacon data for about 7 days and aggregated data for up to six months. Cloudflare may also process ordinary request information such as IP addresses. See Cloudflare's Web Analytics documentation and Cloudflare's Privacy Policy. Our deletion page asks your browser not to run this script
  • Formspree: Our contact form is for parents, carers, teachers, and other adults. When you choose Send Message, your email address, optional name, selected topic, and message are sent to Formspree and delivered to us. Formspree may also process technical request information and spam checks. Simply opening the contact page does not submit a message. See Formspree's Privacy Policy
  • Email: If you contact us by email, including through a mailto link, your email application and email providers process and deliver the message. We receive your address and the information you choose to include
  • External links: Store, book, support, and reference links open other services only when selected. Google Play, Amazon, Ko-fi, and other linked services have their own policies. If you choose to support us through Ko-fi, Ko-fi may share transaction or supporter information with us according to your choices and its privacy policy

Please do not send children's names, email addresses, schools, birth dates, photos, or other identifying details in support messages. A description of the issue and the browser or app version is usually enough.

4. Data Retention

App saves can be removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the app. Browser saves can be removed by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. These actions cannot be undone by us. They do not delete the optional parent purchase account records described in section 2.7, which the upcoming Space Academy update lets a grown-up delete as described in section 2.8 (minimal pseudonymous purchase fingerprints kept 30 days, then erased automatically). We keep adult support correspondence as needed to respond and handle follow-up; you can contact us to request its deletion. Hosting, contact-form, email, store, and Android advertising providers apply their own retention policies.

5. International Users

Hosting, contact-form, email, store, and Android advertising providers may process information in countries other than your own under their policies. Advertising and consent requirements vary by region; website display advertising remains disabled.

6. Your Rights

You can clear local game saves through your browser or device settings. Contact us with privacy questions or requests concerning our services. Requests about information independently processed by third-party providers may also need to be directed to those providers.

7. Data Security

Local game saves depend on your browser and device security. Adult contact messages are handled through the form and email services described above, rather than stored in game saves. No website, device, or service can promise absolute security; avoid including sensitive information in support messages.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. For significant changes, we will make reasonable efforts to provide notice (such as a notice on our website).
