One very silly idea. One unlikely hero.

Your first patrol.

A few controls. A lot of mischief.

01

Take to the skies

Fly with the thumbstick, WASD or arrow keys. The camera follows you across the city.

02

Pick your moment

Tap Drop or press Space to drop straight down. Tap or click a spot in the city to aim a throw.

03

Watch the street

Criminals glow red. Avoid civilians and watch for criminals who shoot back. Use Pause whenever you need a break.
