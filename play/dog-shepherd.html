Dog Shepherd

A very good dog.
A surprisingly tricky job.

You're a Golden Retriever with a flock to protect. The catch? Your bark scares the wolves, but it scares your sheep too.

Play Dog Shepherd ↗
Free / Touch, keyboard & mouseHow to play ↓

Content: wolves chasing sheep and cartoon peril.

A little strategy. A lot of wagging.

Get to know your flock.

Choose your moves, then try a run.

01

Round them up

Move with the thumbstick, WASD or arrow keys. Tap near a sheep to nudge it towards safety.

02

Use your inside bark

Bark scares wolves and sheep. Try Wag, Dash, Treat and Howl too; the buttons show their remaining uses.

03

Make a fetching plan

On a touchscreen, choose Fetch and tap a target. With a mouse, point and press F. Protect the flock through eight waves.

Your flock is waiting.

The game opens on its own page. Choose Start herding to begin. Pause also freezes the between-wave countdown.
