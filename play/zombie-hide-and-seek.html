A changing maze, a few useful hiding places and zombies on your trail. Sneak past, set a trap and make for the glowing exit.

Content: zombie pursuit, dark settings and traps.

Think ahead. Leave quietly.

A quiet plan helps.

Read the controls, or head straight into the maze.

01

Find your bearings

Move with the thumbstick, WASD or arrow keys. The camera follows you; Map shows the whole maze.

02

Stay out of sight

Hold Sneak or Shift to move quietly. Bushes hide you from sight. Watch the route before moving.

03

Make an escape

Place a trap on your current tile, then reach the glowing exit. Pause freezes the action; a new maze starts a new run.

Can you find the exit?

The game opens on its own page. Choose Start maze when you're ready; the How to play button keeps the controls close.
