Learn, race and explore

All 11 guided lessons, practice, four race difficulties, 30 space discoveries, the shop, achievements and parent settings are included. New to clock hands? Follow the telling-time learning path first, or read a parent's guide to teaching a child to tell time.

Honest rewards and limits

Coins and discoveries are earned through play. The original daily race fuel and journey limits apply. Video ads, ad refills and ad hints are unavailable online; lessons and space journeys remain playable when race fuel runs out.

Local saves only

Progress does not sync to Android or another device. Gameplay stops if browser storage fails. There are no analytics or advertising SDKs in this game. See our privacy policy.

Take the practice with you

Make a printable clock practice sheet for the table. You do not need an app to play online; parents who want offline play can also find Clock Master on Google Play.
