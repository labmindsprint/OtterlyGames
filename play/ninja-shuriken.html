Content: cartoon weapons and zombie combat.

A kid's idea. A whole lot of playtesting.

Three things to know.

No lesson required. Jump in when you're ready.

01

Keep moving

Drag the on-screen thumbstick, or use WASD or arrow keys. Shurikens fly in the direction you last moved.

02

Time your throws

Hold Throw on a touchscreen, or press Space. Defeat enemies to unlock powers; each has its own cooldown.

03

Build this run

Spend earned coins in the Garage between levels. This run's progress resets on reload. Use Pause for a break.
