    Chemistry 101, built in

    The game teaches the science as you play. A short guide covers four ideas, using the same atoms you collect.

    What is an atom?

    A core of protons and neutrons, with electrons orbiting it.

    +

    Protons are identity

    Protons decide the element. Electrons fill shells: first 2, then 8, then 8. Hydrogen has 1 proton, helium 2, oxygen 8.

    ▦

    The periodic table

    Sorted by protons. Same column, similar behaviour. Elements in the same column act alike because their atoms have the same number of outer electrons.

    ⌬

    Molecules

    Atoms share electrons to bond. Two H and one O make water. In the Lab, you fuse atoms into molecules, then fit them to your turret.

    Fuse molecules. Power up your turret.

    Pick two atoms and press Fuse. Each molecule fits one of three turret bays: barrel, core or mount, and changes how your tower fights. Try it.
