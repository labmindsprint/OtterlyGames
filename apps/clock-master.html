11 Guided Clock Chapters

Animated explanations lead into hands-on practice. Clear instructions, precise hand dragging, and immediate answer feedback let children learn at their own pace. Move to the next question when ready.

  • Hour Hand — Learn what each number means
  • Minute Hand — Understanding minutes around the clock
  • Reading Time — Combining hours and minutes
  • AM/PM & Day-Night Cycle — Morning vs. evening
  • 24-Hour Clock — Military time made simple
  • Quarter & Half Past — Common time expressions
  • Time Addition — "What time will it be in 2 hours?"
  • Time Subtraction — "What time was it 30 minutes ago?"
  • Clock Angles — See how the hands form angles
  • The Sneaky Hour Hand — Watch the hour hand move between numbers
  • The Chase Game — Explore when clock hands meet

Time Race — Car Racing Game

An exciting racing game where quick, correct answers to time-telling questions give your car a nitro boost! Race against a computer opponent across difficulty levels.

  • 4 difficulty levels — Easy, Medium, Hard, Master
  • Answer time questions to get speed boosts
  • 41 original illustrated cars with animated racing details
  • Daily fuel refills, with optional rewarded videos for more fuel
  • Earn coins for car upgrades and customizations

Explore the Universe

Embark on a cosmic journey through 30 real celestial objects! From Earth to a Black Hole — answer time questions to fly your rocket to each destination and learn amazing space facts.

  • 30 celestial objects — planets, nebulae, galaxies, and more
  • Educational trivia for each space object with TTS narration
  • Progressive difficulty as you travel deeper into space
  • Beautiful space illustrations and animations
  • Combine astronomy learning with clock-reading practice

Meet Otterly and Friends

Otterly is a curious little timekeeper with a pocket watch and a playful personality. Choose from five original 2D companions, each with their own character animation.

  • Otterly, Time Robot, Clock Cat, Space Explorer, and Time Wizard
  • A guided first-time walkthrough with your companion
  • Text-to-speech voice guidance throughout
  • Gentle, encouraging feedback on every answer
  • Parental gate protects settings with a math problem
  • All data stays on device — no accounts needed
