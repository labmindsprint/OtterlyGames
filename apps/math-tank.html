Epic Tank Battles

The core of Math Tank — answer multiplication and division questions to fire missiles at enemy pirate tanks! Faster correct answers mean more powerful shots. Defeat enemies to earn coins for upgrades.

  • 4 selectable difficulty levels — Easy (×1-5), Medium (+division), Hard (×6-10), God (×11-20)
  • 10 unique missiles — Laser Beam, Ice Shard, Lightning Bolt, and more
  • Each missile has 4 power levels based on answer speed
  • Special effects — Freeze, Burn, Chain, Pull, Phase
  • Age-based timer bonuses — gentler for younger players
  • 3 free battles daily; optional ads for extra battles are available only in the Android app
📚✨

6 Animated Math Lessons

Step-by-step animated lessons that teach multiplication and division concepts from scratch. Each lesson includes visual explanations, guided practice, and coin rewards.

  • Multiplication Basics — Groups of items (visual learning)
  • Advanced Multiplication — Extended times tables
  • Column Multiplication — Multi-digit with partial products
  • Division Concept — Sharing equally (visual learning)
  • Long Division — With remainders explained step-by-step
  • Two-Digit Multiplication — Full column method mastery
🚀🌌

Save the Universe — 90 Space Levels

Travel through space fighting pirate tanks to save 30 celestial objects! Three battle phases per object — from Earth all the way to a Black Hole. Learn real astronomy trivia along the way.

  • 30 celestial objects — Earth, Moon, ISS, Sun, all planets, nebulae, galaxies
  • 3 phases per object = 90 total levels
  • Real astronomical facts with TTS narration
  • Progressive difficulty scaling
  • Hint system for tricky questions
  • Rocket travel animations between destinations
🏋️♾️

Unlimited Free Practice

No timer and no fuel cost in Practice mode. Work on multiplication and division at your child's own pace, with time to think through each answer. Battle and Universe modes are timed.

  • Multiplication table practice (choose any table)
  • Division table practice
  • Column multiplication drills
  • Long division (bracket) practice
  • Study mode — view full times tables before practicing
  • Encouraging messages for every answer

  • 12 color skins — Factory, Neon, Galaxy, Rainbow, Camo, and more
  • 11 patterns — Leopard Spot, Pixel Art, Circuit Board, Lava, and more
