About Our Free Learning Tools

These tools offer focused activities to try with your child, with answers checked in the browser. Table practice is untimed; the separate Math Speed Quiz has a countdown. Choose the format that feels comfortable today.

No installation, account, or payment is needed. Interactive tools require JavaScript, and read-aloud features depend on your browser's speech support. Ordinary page requests still go to our hosting provider; see our privacy policy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these learning tools really free?

Yes, free to use with no signup or installation. Pages and assets still download to your browser.

What ages are these tools designed for?

The activities cover a range of early-primary skills. Card ages are suggestions, not assessments or requirements shared by every curriculum. Start with a skill your child is ready to explore.

Do I need to install anything?

No app installation is needed. Use a modern browser with JavaScript enabled; speech and other features may vary by browser and device.
