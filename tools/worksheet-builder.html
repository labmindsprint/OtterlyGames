Free Printable Math Worksheets and Practice Papers

A little practice you can take away from the screen. Choose addition, subtraction, times tables, picture-based multiplication and division, clock reading or a Space Academy question sheet from PP1 to Class 5. Print a clean worksheet, optionally add a separate answer key, or share a link that recreates the same questions.

No account, child names, saved answers or timer. Questions are generated in this browser. A shared link includes only the activity settings and sheet seed; it is not a progress backup. Space Academy sheets are original Otterly practice questions, not an official SOF or CREST paper and not complete exam preparation.

Make a sheet that fits today

A full practice paper has 35 questions for Classes 1 to 4 and 50 for Class 5. PP1 and PP2 have short picture sheets only.

A code for the question order: 1–32 letters, numbers, hyphens or underscores. Do not use a child's name or personal details. The same settings and seed recreate the same sheet.

    OTTERLY GAMES · FOR CHECKING TOGETHER

    Answer key

      Match the activity and sheet seed before checking. A shared link starts with the answer key hidden.

      Use a sheet as a starting point, not a test

      Choose fewer when helpful

      Start with five questions and stop when you have something to discuss. Fixed times tables, divisors and whole-hour clocks have 12 different questions; the picture activities have 16 and 25. Addition, subtraction and Space Academy sheets make new questions from each seed. Five-minute clocks have 144 possible times.

      Check the idea, not the speed

      Use the times-tables, division or telling-time path for a worked example. Division sheets use exact positive whole-number answers only. Clocks use 12-hour faces without AM or PM.

      Where the questions come from

      Times-table and division facts follow Math Tank, including its equal-group and sharing pictures. Addition and subtraction use Otterly's own ranges; Math Tank itself practises multiplication and division. Question sheets come from the same engine as Space Academy, with original diagrams, picture choices and worked explanations in the answer key. They are practice, not an official SOF or CREST paper.

      Keep the same questions

      Build with a chosen seed to return to a sheet, or use Generate fresh sheet for a new seed. The link contains only the activity, its level or table, grade and chapter, question count and seed. It does not store completed answers or create an account. Opening the page still makes a normal request to the website host.
