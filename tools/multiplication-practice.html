Study, Then Try a Few Answers

Study mode shows all 12 facts for a table. Read a few together, discuss an equal-group example, then try Practice mode when your child is ready. The times-tables learning path connects these steps to other activities. For game-style ideas away from the keyboard, see times-tables games for families.

Start with the 2s, 5s and 10s — their patterns are easy to spot and give children quick confidence. Once those are solid, tackle 3s, 4s and 6s. Save the 7s and 12s for last.

Why This Tool Uses Typed Answers

Typing asks the child to produce an answer instead of choosing from a list. That is one kind of practice, not evidence that this tool is better for every learner. Return to Study mode whenever a reference would help.

There’s no timer and no pressure — your child can take as long as they need. Speed comes naturally once the facts are known.

Practice Tips for Parents

Let your child choose which table to practise — ownership increases motivation. Celebrate the score counter rather than speed. Once individual tables are strong, use All Tables (Random) mode for the ultimate test.

Pair digital practice with groups of objects, dice games, or a paper times-tables sheet. You can stop without reaching a particular score or streak.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can we use this times-table tool?

Choose a table, explore its facts in Study mode, then try typed answers in untimed Practice. Return to Study mode or use objects when a fact is confusing. Choose All Tables for mixed practice when ready.

In what order could we learn times tables?

The 2, 5, and 10 tables have patterns you can explore first. Other tables can follow in an order that fits your child's understanding and their teacher's approach. This is a suggestion, not a fixed curriculum.

How long does it take a child to learn times tables?

There is no fixed timetable or measured improvement promised by this tool. Keep practice manageable and check understanding as well as recall. Ask the teacher for help with persistent difficulties.
